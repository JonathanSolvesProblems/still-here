# Still Here: 75-second demo script

Written before the first line of app code, per the focus rule. If the build stops
fitting this script, the build is wrong, not the script.

**Pitch sentence:** Anyone can meet the 516 dogs sitting in the Austin shelter
tonight, hear each one make its own case, and see the 99,905-record reason the
ones at the top have been there so long.

**Genre in three seconds:** it's a days-on-market board, for shelter dogs.

---

### 0:00-0:12 | Cold open on Pancho

*Screen: the board loads. One card fills the frame. A live counter ticks.*

> This is Pancho. He's a white Cairn Terrier in the Austin animal shelter, and
> as of this morning he has been waiting four hundred and forty-eight days.

### 0:12-0:22 | He speaks

*Click Listen. ElevenLabs audio plays over the card.*

> "I'm Pancho. I came in on the twenty-third of May last year. Nobody has
> asked about me since."

### 0:22-0:35 | The board

*Pull back. 516 cards. Scroll. Names, breeds, day counts, all descending.*

> He isn't alone. Five hundred and sixteen dogs have an intake record at
> this shelter and no outcome. Every card is a real animal, pulled live from
> Austin's open data an hour ago. Two hundred and eighteen of them are
> bully-type dogs.

### 0:35-0:50 | The myth

*Cut to the chart. Black bar highlighted.*

> You have probably heard that black dogs get left behind. I checked it
> against ninety-nine thousand nine hundred and five completed shelter stays
> going back to 2013. It isn't true. Black dogs leave in nine days, faster
> than white, brown, or chocolate.

### 0:50-1:05 | The real answer

*Chart splits into the controlled comparison. Two flat rows.*

> Because the thing that actually decides is breed. Hold breed constant and
> colour moves the wait by four days. Change the breed and it moves by twenty.
> A bully-type dog waits twenty-eight days. Every other dog waits eight. Black
> only looked fast because black dogs are the least likely to be pit bulls.

*Beat. Two rows highlight.*

> A black dog that isn't a bully breed waits eight days. A black pit bull
> waits thirty-one.

### 1:05-1:15 | Close on the serve

*Back to the board. Pancho's card. Cursor over Listen.*

> Snowflake does the counting. ElevenLabs gives them the voice. Every dog on
> this board is still there right now, and every one of them can tell you how
> long it's been.

**Rules this script is holding me to**

- Opens on a dog, not an architecture diagram (rule 44).
- Headline is days waited, a world unit about a real animal, never a metric
  about my own code (rules 32, 45, 52).
- Runs on live data fetched the morning of recording, never seeded (rules 31, 48).
- Sponsor tech named once, at the end, as the mechanism (rule 28).
- No proof console as the climax. The climax is Pancho (rule 45).
